INDIGO · MARIGOLD HALO · EN/हिं/मर
Brand Identity
Three elements, one mark
The new logo combines open book (knowledge), rising sun (enlightenment / new day at school), and lotus petals (Indian heritage / blooming child). Every element earns its place.
Palette Philosophy
Saffron + Forest Teal
Saffron #E85D2E = sacred, energetic, Indian flag. Teal #0F766E = classroom blackboard nostalgia, growth, calm. Together they balance energy & learning — perfect for schools.
Marigold Accent
For celebration only
Marigold #FFB800 appears ONLY for achievements, A+ grades, parent role badges, and special notifications. Never for errors. Inspired by marigold garlands at Indian festivals.
Indigo Heritage
Royal scholarly depth
Indigo #2E1A6B serves as primary text color and replaces pure black. Used for bottom nav, profile headers, and serious sections. Connects to traditional Indian textile dyes — gravitas without austerity.
Role-Specific Headers
Each role, its own header
Parent = saffron header (warm welcome). Student = teal (calm focus). Teacher = indigo (professional). Principal = deep saffron (executive). Admin = crimson (authority). Same components, different headers.
Cultural Heritage
विद्या & नमस्ते
Splash shows "विद्या ददाति विनयं" (Knowledge gives humility — ancient Sanskrit). Login shows नमस्ते. Currency uses ₹. EN/हिं/मर toggle in profile. Festival-aware UI possible.
Parent = Marigold
The most important user
In School ERP, parents get marigold — they're the customers paying fees, the decision-makers, the trust holders. Making their role color the celebration accent visibly tells them: "you matter most here."
Accessibility · WCAG AA
For everyone
Indigo text on ivory: 13.4:1 (AAA). Saffron buttons with white text: 4.6:1 (AA). 48dp touch targets, 16sp body min. Patterns/icons accompany every status color — works for elderly and color-blind users.
